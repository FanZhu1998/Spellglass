Tip finder

The tracking test from the Spellglass blueprint (Appendix A): can your camera see the wand tip?

The camera image stays on this computer. Values you set here are saved as Spellglass's starting values in this browser, so the app begins where you leave off. A camera profile saved in Wand tuning still wins for that camera.

The test, step by step

One evening. You need the wand, a phone with a flashlight, a room you can dim, and this page.

  1. Do: Room lights on. Hold the wand tip 1 m from the camera, pointed at it.

    Look for: whether the tip stands out at all. It probably will not.

  2. Do: Hold the phone flashlight so it touches the bezel next to the webcam. Dim the room.

    Look for: a sharp bright dot at the tip that follows the wand.

  3. Do: Slide the flashlight 10 cm away from the lens.

    Look for: the dot should fade. If it does, the tip sends light straight back to its source, and tracking with a light beside the lens works.

  4. Do: Flashlight off. Turn on the white band below, with the screen at full brightness.

    Look for: a fainter dot. If it is usable, no extra light is needed.

  5. Do: Lower the exposure until the room is nearly black.

    Look for: the contrast readout. Aim for the tip to be 3 times brighter than the brightest background, or more.

  6. Do: Draw circles and flicks at a natural pace.

    Look for: the tip found in 9 frames out of 10, and streaks shorter than the dot is wide.

  7. Do: Repeat steps 5 and 6 at 0.6 m and at 1.5 m.

    Look for: the range where they still pass. Record a 10 s session at each distance.

  8. Do: Point a TV remote at the webcam and press a button.

    Look for: a visible flash. Then the camera passes some infrared, and an infrared LED beside the lens may work without a new camera.

  9. Do: Open the camera list on this page.

    Look for: a second camera with "IR" in its name. It is marked as infrared.

  10. Do: Open Google's hand-landmark demo and hold the wand as you would to cast.

    Look for: whether the landmarks stay on your hand through a few gestures.
